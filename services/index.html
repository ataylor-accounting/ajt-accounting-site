Services

Accounting services that keep your finances organized and useful.

Bookkeeping Services

Organized books are the backbone of any business. Monthly bookkeeping, bank and credit card reconciliations, and financial reports keep your records current and ready for tax season, audits, and planning.

  • Monthly transaction categorization
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Balance sheet, profit and loss, and cash flow reporting
  • Tax-ready records

Tax Preparation Services

AJT Accounting prepares individual and business tax returns with planning support designed to reduce stress and help you understand your filing position.

  • Individual and business returns
  • Tax planning strategies
  • IRS correspondence support
  • S-corporation elections and transitions

Payroll Services

Payroll support helps businesses pay employees accurately and stay compliant with payroll tax requirements.

  • Payroll processing support
  • Quarterly and year-end reporting
  • W-2, 1099, and related document support
  • Employee benefit tracking support

Financial Cleanup Services

If books are months or years behind, AJT Accounting can organize records, correct errors, reconcile accounts, and restore financial data you can trust.

  • Tax-ready financials
  • Improved organization going forward

Financial consulting
