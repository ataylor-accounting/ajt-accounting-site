About AJT Accounting

Local expertise. Nationwide reach.

Founded in Highland, Indiana, AJT Accounting provides practical accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, and tax support for individuals and small businesses.

Clear financial records make stronger decisions possible.

AJT Accounting is built on a simple goal: helping individuals and businesses feel confident and clear about their finances.

Over the past five years, Avery Taylor, CPA has supported clients with accounting cleanup, tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll, and planning ahead with reliable accounting support they can trust as they grow.

Clients are more than numbers. They are entrepreneurs, families, and driven professionals. AJT Accounting provides the financial clarity that helps them move forward with confidence.
